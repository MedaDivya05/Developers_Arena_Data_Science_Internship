# 1. Overview

This document presents the customer segment profiles created as part of the Week 11 project: **Customer Segmentation & Prediction**.

Customer segmentation was performed using clustering techniques to group customers based on similar behavior and characteristics. The purpose of segmentation is to understand different customer groups, identify high-value customers, detect churn-prone customers, and create targeted business strategies.

The segmentation analysis was performed using customer churn data and machine learning techniques such as:

- K-Means Clustering
- Agglomerative Clustering
- Feature Scaling
- Segment Profiling
- Random Forest Prediction Models

---

# 2. Objective of Customer Segmentation

The main objectives of customer segmentation are:

- Identify different types of customers based on behavior
- Understand customer spending patterns
- Detect customers with high churn risk
- Create segment-specific business strategies
- Improve customer retention
- Support personalized marketing campaigns
